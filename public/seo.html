ClientWorks Login by LPL Financial

Sign in to ClientWorks, LPL Financial's integrated advisor workstation and the operational hub of LPL Latitude. One secure login connects the tools, data, and workflows advisors rely on every day — from portfolio management and trading to client reporting, financial planning, and practice management.

ClientWorks is built for independent financial advisors, registered investment advisors (RIAs), and wealth management professionals. As the nation's largest independent broker-dealer and a leading RIA custodian, LPL Financial supports approximately 22,000 financial advisors and more than 1,100 institution-based investment programs through the ClientWorks platform. ClientWorks was first piloted in 2015 to replace LPL's legacy BranchNet system, and it remains the central, cloud-based home for the modern advisory practice.

How to Log In to ClientWorks

Accessing ClientWorks is fast and secure. Choose the sign-in method that fits your workflow:

  1. Open the ClientWorks login page and confirm you are on the official LPL Financial site.
  2. Enter your username and password, then select Sign In.
  3. Verify your identity with multi-factor authentication — an authenticator app, SMS code, or hardware security key.
  4. Optional — sign in with a QR code. Returning users can scan the QR code with the ClientWorks mobile app on a registered device for a faster, passwordless login.
  5. You're in. Manage portfolios, trade, generate client reports, and plan — all from one secure login.

First time or using a new device? Select Register New Device on the login page, log in with your username and password, and complete the shown authentication steps to enable QR code sign-in. Need help? See the QR Code Instructions.

ClientWorks Login Options

ClientWorks supports flexible, secure sign-in options so you can access your practice from anywhere:

  • Username & Password: Sign in with your assigned credentials through the secure login portal.
  • QR Code / Passwordless Login: Scan the code with a registered mobile device and confirm with your device's biometric or PIN — no password and no SMS/call-based codes required.
  • Register New Device: Add and secure a new device in a few steps, then unlock passwordless sign-in.
  • On-The-Go Mode: Toggle On-The-Go to mask sensitive information on the screen when you step away, helping protect client data on public or shared computers.
  • Single Sign-On (SSO): Log in once and move seamlessly between ClientWorks and connected tools such as Wealthbox, Salesforce, eMoney's WealthVision, and Morningstar's Advisor Workstation.

Security & Compliance

ClientWorks is built on LPL Latitude, the security and data architecture behind modern advice, and implements enterprise-grade measures to protect sensitive financial data and maintain regulatory compliance:

  • Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Required for advisor logins with support for authenticator apps, SMS verification, hardware security keys, and passwordless/passkey sign-in.
  • Encryption: Data protected in transit and at rest using industry-standard encryption.
  • Role-Based Access Controls: Granular permissions for team members, branch offices, and supervisory hierarchies.
  • Session & Device Security: Automatic timeout, device registration and tracking, and suspicious-activity monitoring.
  • Regulatory Compliance: Built-in workflows aligned with SEC, FINRA, and state regulatory requirements.
  • Data Loss Prevention & Audit Trail: Policies to prevent unauthorized data export, plus comprehensive logging for compliance review and supervisory oversight.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is ClientWorks?
ClientWorks is LPL Financial's integrated advisor workstation and the operational hub of LPL Latitude. It brings together portfolio management, trading, client reporting, financial planning, and practice management in a single, connected experience with one secure login.
Who can access ClientWorks?
ClientWorks is available to financial advisors and their authorized staff who are affiliated with LPL Financial. Access requires proper credentialing through your LPL Financial relationship.
How do I log in to ClientWorks?
Go to the ClientWorks login page, enter your username and password, and complete multi-factor authentication. You can also log in with a QR code using a registered mobile device for a faster, passwordless experience.
How do I log in to ClientWorks with a QR code?
On the login page, choose the QR code option and scan it with the ClientWorks mobile app on a registered device. This passwordless method uses your device's biometric or PIN authentication and removes the need for SMS or call-based codes.
How do I register a new device in ClientWorks?
On the ClientWorks login page, select Register New Device, then log in with your username and password and complete the authentication steps shown. After that, the device can use QR code sign-in.
What should I do if I forget my ClientWorks password?
Click the "Forgot Password" link on the login page and follow the prompts to verify your identity and reset your password. Contact LPL Technology Support if you need additional assistance.
What is On-The-Go in ClientWorks?
On-The-Go masks sensitive information on the screen when you step away, helping protect client data on shared or public devices. Toggle it on or off from the login screen.
Is ClientWorks available on mobile devices?
Yes. The ClientWorks mobile app is available for iOS and Android. It lets you view portfolios, access client information, approve transactions, use QR code login, and send secure messages.
How does LPL protect my data in ClientWorks?
ClientWorks uses multi-factor authentication, encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access controls, and continuous monitoring. It supports passwordless and passkey login and adheres to industry security standards.
What is LPL Latitude?
LPL Latitude is LPL Financial's unified technology platform and security architecture. ClientWorks is the advisor-facing hub of LPL Latitude, connecting the data, security, and intelligence behind modern advice.
