ClientWorks is LPL Financial's integrated technology platform designed for independent financial advisors, registered investment advisors (RIAs), and wealth management professionals. As the nation's largest independent broker-dealer, LPL Financial serves over 22,000 financial advisors across the United States through its ClientWorks platform.

The ClientWorks portal delivers a unified experience for managing client relationships, executing trades, monitoring portfolios, and running a financial advisory practice. Built on modern cloud infrastructure, ClientWorks provides real-time access to the tools and information advisors need to serve their clients effectively.

Platform Features & Capabilities

Portfolio Management

Comprehensive portfolio management with real-time performance tracking, asset allocation analysis, rebalancing tools, and customizable model portfolios. Monitor individual and household accounts with consolidated views.

Trading & Order Management

Execute equity, fixed income, mutual fund, and ETF trades through an integrated order management system. Access block trading, trade blotters, and automated rebalancing capabilities.

Client Reporting

Generate professional client reports with customizable templates. Performance reporting, account statements, tax documents, and ad-hoc reporting tools for comprehensive client communication.

Financial Planning

Integrated financial planning tools for retirement projections, education funding, insurance analysis, and comprehensive financial plans. Monte Carlo simulations and goal-based planning modules.

Account Management

Open new accounts, process transfers, manage beneficiary designations, and handle account maintenance. Digital document management with e-signature capabilities for paperless workflows.

Practice Management

Business analytics, client segmentation, workflow automation, and productivity tools. Track key practice metrics, manage client relationships, and optimize business operations.

Security & Compliance

ClientWorks implements enterprise-grade security measures to protect sensitive financial data and maintain regulatory compliance:

  • Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Required for all advisor logins with support for authenticator apps, SMS verification, and hardware security keys
  • 256-bit AES Encryption: All data encrypted at rest and in transit using industry-standard encryption protocols
  • SOC 2 Type II Compliance: Regular third-party audits ensure adherence to security, availability, and confidentiality standards
  • Role-Based Access Controls: Granular permission management for team members, branch offices, and supervisory hierarchies
  • Session Management: Automatic timeout, device tracking, and suspicious activity monitoring
  • Regulatory Compliance: Built-in compliance workflows for SEC, FINRA, and state regulatory requirements
  • Data Loss Prevention: Policies to prevent unauthorized data export and maintain information barriers
  • Audit Trail: Comprehensive logging of all user actions for compliance review and supervisory oversight

Account Types Supported

ClientWorks supports a full range of account types for individual and institutional clients:

  • Individual & Joint Accounts: Taxable brokerage accounts, individual retirement accounts (IRA), Roth IRA, SEP IRA, and SIMPLE IRA
  • Trust Accounts: Revocable living trusts, irrevocable trusts, testamentary trusts, and charitable remainder trusts
  • Corporate Accounts: Business accounts, corporate investment accounts, and cash management solutions
  • Retirement Plans: 401(k), 403(b), defined benefit plans, and profit-sharing plans
  • Education Accounts: 529 college savings plans, Coverdell Education Savings Accounts, and UGMA/UTMA custodial accounts
  • Advisory Accounts: Fee-based advisory programs, managed account platforms, and model portfolio strategies

Getting Started with ClientWorks

System Requirements

ClientWorks is a web-based application optimized for modern browsers including Google Chrome (recommended), Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, and Apple Safari. A stable internet connection is required for real-time data access and trading capabilities.

Advisors access ClientWorks using their assigned credentials through the secure login portal. Multi-factor authentication is required for all accounts to ensure the highest level of security. QR code login is available for returning users with the ClientWorks mobile app.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is ClientWorks?
ClientWorks is LPL Financial's primary technology platform for financial advisors. It provides tools for portfolio management, trading, client reporting, financial planning, and practice management in a single integrated environment.
Who can access ClientWorks?
ClientWorks is available to financial advisors and their authorized staff who are affiliated with LPL Financial. Access is granted through your LPL Financial relationship and requires proper credentialing.
How do I log in to ClientWorks?
Visit the ClientWorks login page and enter your username and password. You'll be prompted for multi-factor authentication to verify your identity. QR code login is also available through the ClientWorks mobile app.
What should I do if I forget my password?
Click the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. Follow the instructions to verify your identity and reset your password. Contact LPL Technology Support if you need additional assistance.
Is ClientWorks available on mobile devices?
Yes, ClientWorks Mobile is available for iOS and Android devices through the respective app stores. The mobile app provides access to key features including portfolio monitoring, client information, and secure messaging.
How does LPL protect my data?
ClientWorks uses 256-bit encryption, multi-factor authentication, role-based access controls, and continuous monitoring to protect your data. The platform undergoes regular SOC 2 Type II audits and complies with industry security standards.
Can I customize my ClientWorks experience?
Yes, ClientWorks offers customizable dashboards, report templates, and workflow configurations. You can personalize your home screen, create custom watchlists, and set up automated alerts based on your preferences.
What integrations are available?
ClientWorks integrates with major CRM platforms (Salesforce, Redtail, Wealthbox), financial planning tools (MoneyGuidePro, eMoney), and performance reporting platforms (Orion, Black Diamond). Additional third-party integrations are available through the LPL marketplace.
